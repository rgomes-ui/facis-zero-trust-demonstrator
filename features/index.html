Features and journeys¶

The successful journey¶

A participant backend in zone A calls a protected resource in zone B. Every hop is checked: workload identity in the mesh, admission control on what was allowed to run at all, a policy decision at the guard, an attested channel between the zones, and a token that was minted against a verified credential presentation rather than a shared secret.

The refusal journeys¶

Refusals are demonstrated as deliberately as successes:

  • a revoked credential — verification flips negative, the grant refuses, the token store fails closed;
  • a wrong scope — the guard denies and returns the reason and an OID4VP link;
  • a tampered measurement — the attested handshake aborts before any traffic flows.
